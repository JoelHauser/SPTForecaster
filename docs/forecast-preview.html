Glyphs

Taken from SPT

  • Tarkov time is real time × 7 plus 3 hours; the right-hand slot is 12 hours ahead (WeatherHelper).
  • Night is after 21:00 and before 05:00.
  • Preset weights 13 / 7 / 7 (Sunny, Rainy, Cloudy), lowered when a preset repeats, refilled when one runs out.
  • Cloud, rain, fog and wind values per preset from weather.json; raid lengths from each map's base.json.

Proposed by the mod

  • Schedule keyed to the Tarkov clock instead of the real clock, so the two slots differ.
  • Saved to disk so a restart keeps it, and the raid's weather locked when the slot is confirmed.
  • No forecast on Factory, The Lab, Labyrinth or Icebreaker; Icebreaker shows one slot at 23:00.
  • The location map is a schematic, and the cut-offs that turn numbers into words are first guesses.
