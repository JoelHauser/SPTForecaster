Taken from SPT

  • Tarkov time is real time × 7 plus 3 hours; the right-hand slot is 12 hours ahead (WeatherHelper).
  • Night is after 21:00 and before 05:00.
  • The game samples its weather curve with the raid's in-game clock, so a location's samples become the raid's weather nodes directly.
  • Value ranges match weather.json: clouds −1 to 1.2, rain levels 1–5, fog 0.0013 upward, wind 0–4.
  • Raid lengths from each map's base.json.

How the field works

  • Every 8 in-game hours the region rolls a weather pattern: fair weather, unsettled, prolonged rain, a storm front, or coastal fog. Weather already in the sky carries over, so patterns hand over gradually.
  • Storms have shapes: small lumpy showers; fronts and squall lines as long curved bands across the wind, sharp in front with rain trailing behind; a wide, slow shield of prolonged rain; fog lying along the coast. Noise roughens every edge and breaks rain into cells.
  • Each location samples the field at its marker every 14 in-game minutes (two real minutes). The cards, the radar and the raid all read those samples.
  • Everything comes from one seed, so saving the seed is all a server restart needs.
  • Thresholds have a little hysteresis so a cell edge passing over a map doesn't flicker the forecast.
  • The radar is a light wash with fine outlines at each rain level, so the map art and TushonkaTerritories' faction colours show through it.
