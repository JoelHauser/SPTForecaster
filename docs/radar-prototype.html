How the field works

  • The region is always in one weather pattern (fair weather, overcast, showers, prolonged rain, a storm front, coastal fog), in 8-in-game-hour steps. Patterns follow on from each other like real weather: fine spells last, and a break goes cloud, then rain or a front, then showers, then clearing. SPT's season sets how long each lasts and what comes next, modelled on St Petersburg's climate (the real one nearest Tarkov's Norvinsk region).
  • Summer showers come mostly in the afternoon; fog forms on calm nights, more after rain; the wind is lighter in fine weather and stronger with fronts.
  • Storms have shapes: small lumpy showers; fronts and squall lines as long curved bands across the wind, sharp in front with rain trailing behind; a wide, slow shield of prolonged rain; fog lying along the coast. Noise roughens every edge and breaks rain into cells.
  • Each location samples the field at its marker every 14 in-game minutes (two real minutes). The cards, the radar and the raid all read those samples.
  • Everything comes from one seed, so saving the seed is all a server restart needs.
  • Thresholds have a little hysteresis so a cell edge passing over a map doesn't flicker the forecast.
  • The radar is a light wash, one colour per rain level the card names (switching exactly where the card's level does), so the colour over a map is the word on its card and the map art and TushonkaTerritories' faction colours show through.
